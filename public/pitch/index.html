The problem

Three things nobody in Mumbai can see today

📍

Who owns my complaint?

A flooded road is reported and disappears. No owner, no deadline, and it can be "closed" without any proof of a fix.

₹

Where did the money go?

₹13.35 Cr billed for road repairs in one monsoon. Some bills are up to 3× the city's own rate card, and some repairs fail within 30 days.

🛣️

When does the road's guarantee end?

Concrete roads carry a 10-year free-repair guarantee. 493 bills in the dataset were paid while that guarantee was still running.

Figures from the organisers' synthetic dataset (Claude-Impact-Labs-data-2), not BMC records.

Who uses it

One sign-in, five roles

🧑
Resident

Reports a problem, follows it to a verified fix

👷
Ward officer

Works the ward queue, records action, uploads photo proof

🏢
Ward office

Answers public questions about its spending, by a deadline

⚖️
Auditor

Reviews responses independently and publishes the decision

🛡️
Super admin

Command centre, assigns officers, verifies every closure

Each role sees only its own data, enforced in the database. On the demo site, one click signs you in as any role.
