CivicOps Mumbai

Report a civic problem. See who owns it, when it is due, and the proof that it was fixed.

Every report gets a ward, a responsible team, a visible deadline and a timestamped history. Closure needs evidence and a recorded human decision.

Report an issue Trace a historical repair (Link Road, Juhu)

What you can report

  • Waterlogging or flooded road
  • Fallen tree or unsafe branch
  • Burst pipe, leak or water outage
  • Missed garbage pickup or overflowing bin
  • Illegal dumping or bulk waste
  • Blocked drain, sewer overflow or open manhole
  • Pothole, damaged road or broken footpath
  • Streetlight or damaged pole
  • Public spaces and other issues

Who owns it

Location → ward (24 wards) → department by configurable rules. Unclear locations go to a visible triage queue.

When it is due

Red critical: first action within 5 hours. Yellow medium: resolve by day 7. Orange standard: resolve by day 10. Missed deadlines escalate automatically.

Proof it was fixed

Before/after photos, a Claude-assisted comparison, and a super admin decision that is recorded with a reason.
